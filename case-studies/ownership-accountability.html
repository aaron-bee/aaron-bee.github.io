CASE STUDY · 04

Cloud Security Ownership & Risk Accountability

Clarifying who remediates, validates, governs, and accepts risk across shared environments.

Focus
Shared Responsibility · RACI · Remediation Ownership · Risk Acceptance

Environment
Cloud · SaaS · Enterprise infrastructure · Application environments

Role
Governance design, ownership clarification, risk accountability, and cross-team coordination

Challenge

Security issues frequently cross organizational boundaries. Without clear accountability, findings stall and risk ownership becomes ambiguous.

My Role

A recurring part of my work is determining who fixes an issue, who validates the requirement, who governs the process, and who accepts residual risk.

What I Did

  • Built or contributed to RACI structures and ownership models.
  • Clarified responsibilities across security, infrastructure, application, identity, architecture, and business teams.
  • Used exception and risk workflows to establish remediation and acceptance ownership.
  • Supported tracking and follow-through so findings did not remain ownerless.

Key Decisions

  • Assign one clearly accountable owner for each control or risk decision.
  • Separate security governance from implementation ownership.
  • Keep application-specific risk with the appropriate system or business owner.

Outcome

The work helped reduce ambiguity in control ownership, improved accountability for remediation and exceptions, and created clearer paths to resolve or formally accept risk.

Leadership Takeaway

The value of a RACI is organizational agreement on who owns the control, remediation, and risk.
