CASE STUDY · 02

Kubernetes Shared Responsibility RACI

Clarifying who owns security decisions across platform, application, infrastructure, and governance teams.

Focus
Kubernetes · Containers · Operating model · RACI

Environment
Enterprise container platforms · Cloud and hybrid

Role
Security leadership, architecture, governance, and risk translation

Challenge

Container platforms blur traditional ownership boundaries. Without explicit accountability, security gaps appear around image lifecycle, runtime controls, IAM, patching, networking, logging, and workload configuration.

Approach

Break the platform into control domains and assign accountable, responsible, consulted, and informed roles. Separate control-plane, data-plane, workload, image, identity, and monitoring responsibilities.

Security principles

  • One accountable owner per control area
  • Platform and workload responsibilities are distinct
  • Security sets guardrails and assurance requirements
  • Application teams retain ownership of application-specific risk

Outcome

A practical responsibility model that reduces duplicated work, closes ownership gaps, and gives engineering teams a clear path for secure deployment and remediation.

Leadership takeaway

The most valuable part of a RACI is not the table itself. It is the conversation that forces teams to make hidden assumptions about ownership explicit.
